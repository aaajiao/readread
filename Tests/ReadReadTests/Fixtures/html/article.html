The Talking Machine

By Ada Example

Speech synthesis is the artificial production of human speech. A computer system used for this purpose is called a speech synthesizer, and it can be built in software or in hardware.[1]

Early machines

Long before electronic signal processing, inventors tried to build machines that imitate the human voice. Some of them used bellows, reeds and leather tubes to shape the sound.

  • The acoustic-mechanical speech machine of the eighteenth century.
  • The Voder, shown at the 1939 World's Fair.

Modern systems

Modern systems join recorded units or generate the waveform with a neural network. They read news, books and screens aloud for people who cannot or would rather not read them.

The best voice is the one you stop noticing.
