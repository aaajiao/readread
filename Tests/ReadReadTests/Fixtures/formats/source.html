A Short History of Talking Machines

Speech synthesis is the artificial production of human speech. A computer system used for this purpose is called a speech synthesizer.

Early machines

Long before electronic signal processing, inventors built machines that imitated the human voice with bellows, reeds and leather tubes.

  • The acoustic-mechanical speech machine of the eighteenth century.
  • The Voder, shown at the 1939 World's Fair.
